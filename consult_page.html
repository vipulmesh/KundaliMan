Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your consultation.

Is my birth time necessary?

Yes — accurate birth time significantly improves the precision of your chart reading. The Ascendant (Lagna), house positions, and dasha timings all depend on the exact time of birth. Even a 15-minute difference can shift key placements.

What if I don't know my exact birth time?

A consultation can still be conducted, though the precision of house-related predictions may be reduced. We use Chandra Lagna (Moon sign) and other techniques to provide meaningful guidance even without exact birth time.

How will the consultation happen?

Through your selected call method — WhatsApp call or audio call — at the booked time slot. You'll receive a WhatsApp message confirming the details before your session.

Is my information confidential?

Absolutely. All birth details and personal information remain completely private. Your data is never shared with third parties or used for any purpose beyond your consultation.

How long is the consultation?

It depends on the plan you choose: the Starter session runs about 20 minutes, the Kundali Report gives you a detailed PDF, and the Premium relationship consultation typically runs 30 to 45 minutes. You're encouraged to prepare your key questions in advance.

What astrology system is used?

Traditional Vedic astrology (Jyotish), using the sidereal zodiac, divisional charts (Navamsha, Dashamsha, etc.), and Vimshottari dasha system for timing predictions.

What if I miss the scheduled call?

If you miss the scheduled time, please message on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to reschedule to the next available slot. Kindly notify at least 2 hours in advance if you need to reschedule.

Is there a refund policy?

If the consultation call is not received from our side for any reason, a complete refund will be issued — no questions asked. Your trust and satisfaction is our highest priority.
